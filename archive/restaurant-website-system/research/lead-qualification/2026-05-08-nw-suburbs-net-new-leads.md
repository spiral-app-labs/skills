# Northwest Chicago suburbs net-new restaurant lead qualification — 2026-05-08

Scope: safe, non-mutating public-source lead research for Spiral's restaurant website agency around Schaumburg / northwest Chicago suburbs. I avoided the explicitly stale/blocked names in the brief and also avoided the fresh 2026-05-07 report candidates I found locally (`Sorry Mommy`, `Maxfield's Pancake House`, `Casa Di Luigi`, `Noodle Deli`, `Bacowka`).

Agency routing rule used: each candidate below has one likely archetype fit and a truthful conversion path based only on public evidence. No MC/Supabase writes were made.

## Top two to seed next

1. **Barakat Restaurant — Elk Grove Village**: strongest visible web gap. The official site is essentially a thin Lovable shell while public sources show reservation/order intent and a distinctive Central Asian / European food story.
2. **The Chef Grill — Elk Grove Village**: strong cuisine-specific build material, but the official site/menu extraction is shallow; a Cuisine-led build could clarify menu, order, catering, and halal/Turkish differentiation.

## 1. Barakat Restaurant

- **Location:** Elk Grove Village, IL — public ordering source lists `1090 Landmeier Road, Elk Grove Village, IL`.
- **Official / public URLs:**
  - Official site: https://barakatplace.com/
  - OpenTable public listing: https://www.opentable.com/r/barakat-restaurant-elk-grove-village
  - Toast ordering: https://www.toasttab.com/local/order/barakat-restaurant
  - Chicago Northwest listing: https://www.chicagonorthwest.com/listing/barakat-restaurant/6434/
  - Yelp listing: https://www.yelp.com/biz/barakat-restaurant-elk-grove-village
- **Why sellable:** Distinctive category and story: search/OpenTable snippets describe Central Asian traditions with European sophistication, menu examples like plov, manti, lagman, grilled steaks, and fresh pasta. That gives a preview real narrative depth instead of generic restaurant copy.
- **Visible website gap / opportunity:** Official homepage fetch exposed only `Barakat` plus an `Edit with Lovable` badge. Raw HTML includes placeholder comments like `TODO: Set the document title to the name of your application`, `author=Lovable`, and no readable menu/reserve/order/location content. That is a very clean agency pitch: turn a placeholder AI shell into a restaurant website that actually converts.
- **Likely archetype:** **Bamzi** — cinematic dark/light story pacing fits a distinctive Central Asian + European dinner concept better than a warm generic neighborhood layout.
- **Truthful conversion path:** Primary: reserve a table / walk in. Secondary: Toast pickup ordering. Site should route to OpenTable/Google reserve and Toast, while explaining signature dishes and address clearly.
- **Evidence links:** Official site, OpenTable listing, Toast listing, Chicago Northwest listing, Yelp listing above; web fetch of official site showed only title/badge; search result for Toast showed pickup from `1090 Landmeier Road`.
- **Risks / unknowns:** Official site may be intentionally in-progress or recently launched; need verify current hours, menu, owner/contact name, reservation provider, and photo permissions. Public sources disagree slightly on cuisine framing (`Central Asian/European` vs `Middle Eastern`).
- **Recommendation:** **Build** — best net-new seed candidate from this pass.

That is enough identity to build a truthful, appetizing preview.

- **Truthful conversion path:** Primary: order pickup/delivery via order.online/Grubhub/Uber Eats. Secondary: dine-in directions/call and catering/group meals if verified.
- **Evidence links:** Official homepage/menu pages plus order.online, Grubhub, and Uber Eats links above; web fetch captured halal/Turkish/Mediterranean positioning and menu-category-only extraction.
- **Risks / unknowns:** Need full current menu/prices, hours, direct phone, current ordering preference, owner/contact name, and whether catering exists. The homepage may hide more menu content behind scripts that extraction missed.
- **Recommendation:** **Build** — strong second seed candidate after Barakat.
