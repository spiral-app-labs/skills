# The Chef Grill Battle Cards

## One-Sentence Positioning

A warm, menu-forward redesign that makes The Chef Grill’s halal Turkish/Mediterranean food, 807-review proof, and order/call/directions paths obvious before a guest decides where to eat.

## Owner Talking Points

- “We kept the restaurant’s real operating paths instead of replacing them: order online, view the menu, call, and get directions.”
- “The redesign leads with what guests already love in reviews — halal authenticity, Turkish hospitality, fresh bread, grilled meats, mixed grill, pide/lahmacun, and desserts.”
- “The menu is easier to browse on mobile, especially for guests choosing between kebabs, brick oven items, soups/breakfast, family options, and desserts.”
- “The review proof is anonymous and careful: short Google-review fragments, no fake names or invented testimonials.”
- “The concierge is intentionally conservative. It can recommend menu directions and explain halal/contact/order basics, but it tells guests to call for hours, groups, allergies, and availability.”

## Demo Path

1. **Homepage hero:** Show halal Turkish/Mediterranean positioning, review proof, and immediate order/menu/call/directions actions.
2. **Menu shortcuts / inline menu:** Show how quickly a guest can find kebabs, brick oven, breakfast/soups, family options, and desserts.
3. **Anonymous review carousel:** Explain the proof is from Google-review themes without exposing names or inventing quotes.
4. **Sticky mobile CTA:** Show the practical mobile conversion path: Order Online, View Menu, Call, Directions.
5. **Contact / concierge:** Show the contact section and ask the concierge about halal/menu/group planning to demonstrate safe caveats.

## Proof Points To Cite

- Current site has real content and identity, but audit found above-fold conversion, mobile readability, menu scanability, copy polish, and order-path clarity issues.
- Google evidence captured: 30 written reviews from the Highest filter; visible 4.7 stars / 807 reviews.
- Top-three improvements implemented: anonymous review proof carousel, menu shortcut chips, mobile conversion reliability.
- Concierge tests passed for menu recommendation, halal, hours caveat, group/reservation caveat, and allergy safety.
- Build/typecheck passed in the fork/build, improvement, top-three, and concierge evidence artifacts.

## Known Risks / Caveats

- Day-by-day and holiday hours are not verified; do not publish exact hours beyond verified source evidence without owner confirmation.
- Catering/private event capacity is not verified; use “call to plan” until confirmed.
- Preferred ordering provider should be confirmed before launch.
- Owner/founder story and spelling should be verified before making it a core sales claim.
- Current evidence is local; public/shareable deployment still needs QA and packaging.

## Strongest Close

“This does not ask you to change what already works — it makes the food, trust, and next step clearer for the guest who is deciding on their phone.”
