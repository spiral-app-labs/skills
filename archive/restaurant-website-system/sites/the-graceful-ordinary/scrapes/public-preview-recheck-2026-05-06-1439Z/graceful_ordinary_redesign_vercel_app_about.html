How We Began

A labor of love in the community they call home

For Chef Chris Curren and his wife Megan, The Graceful Ordinary opened in late summer 2021 as a new opportunity to do what they love in the community where they live — downtown St. Charles, IL on the banks of the Fox River.

The result is an elevated riverfront concept that earned AAA Three-Diamond recognition — a refined American tavern with a rotating seasonal menu delivered from a wood-burning hearth, a thoughtfully curated wine program, and inventive cocktails.

Grace in the ordinary. That philosophy shapes every decision — from the sourcing of ingredients to the warmth of the welcome.

The Team

The people behind the experience

Executive Chef & Owner

Chris Curren

Award-winning chef and Fox Valley native. Chris and his wife Megan opened The Graceful Ordinary to do what they love in the community where they live — refined rustic cooking delivered fresh from a wood-burning hearth.

Wine Director & Asst. General Manager

Jack Sonin

A lifelong Fox Valley native, Jack earned his Introductory Sommelier Certificate at 21. Formerly of Michelin-Starred Oriole, Kumiko, and acclaimed Tied House — now curating TGO's thoughtful new and old-world wine program.

Pastry Chef

Martina Preradovic

Born and raised in suburban Chicago's Fox Valley, Martina brings a predisposition toward the creative to TGO's pastry program — inventive yet approachable interpretations of classic desserts that mirror Chef Curren's refined rustic style.

What Drives Us
