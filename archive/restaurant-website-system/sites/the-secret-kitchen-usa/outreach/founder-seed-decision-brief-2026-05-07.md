## Why this is sellable

The Secret Kitchen USA is a strong aesthetic/story lead, not a normal “bad website” rescue. The restaurant already has a premium concept: Chef Aanal Kotak, an international The Secret Kitchen presence, a Schaumburg U.S. debut, handmade-spice / royal-kitchen inspiration, a reservation path, and a full modern Indian menu with theatrical dishes.

The opportunity is that the website does not yet make the U.S. debut feel as cinematic or reservation-worthy as the concept. The public facts are compelling, but the current web experience is sparse in the hero/story path and list-heavy in the menu path. A Bamzi preview can make the restaurant feel like a destination: dark visual drama, chef credibility, curated menu journey, and a sticky reserve/visit path.

## Pitch angle

“Your U.S. debut already has the story. The website should make it feel like an occasion — and make reservations, menu discovery, and directions effortless.”

## Why Bamzi, not Roma or Heaven Palate

Bamzi fits because The Secret Kitchen’s strongest public cues are cinematic and high-drama: “Flavors of the World / From India to America,” Chef Aanal’s royal-kitchen inspiration, handmade spices, jewel-tone visual direction, a substantial global/modern Indian menu, and dessert theatre like Gulab Jamun Flambé.

Roma would make the restaurant too restrained and quiet. Heaven Palate would push it toward classic luxury/steakhouse formality and lose the modern Indian/global-spice rhythm. Cuisine would improve clarity, but it would underuse the brand’s destination-dining drama. Bamzi gives the right structure: dark-to-light-to-dark pacing, poster hero, editorial story sections, menu rhythm breaks, and a strong reservation CTA.

## What a preview should prove

1. The first screen can feel like a premium U.S. debut, not a generic Indian restaurant landing page.
2. Chef Aanal’s story can become a trust/identity anchor without turning into an awards wall.
3. The long menu can become a curated journey: street food reimagined, first plates, royal mains, sweet theatre, bread/comfort details.
4. The reserve/visit path can be clearer than the current repeated nav/footer experience.
5. The design can feel visually rich while staying factual and avoiding invented claims.

## Why this is not build-ready yet

- The lead has not been officially seeded into MC because protected MC bearer auth is unavailable in the heartbeat environment.
- Canonical website workflow root/child tasks do not exist yet for this lead.
- Owner photo rights are unconfirmed.
- Menu currentness/prices need confirmation before a public preview publishes a full menu.
- Private dining/banquet details need confirmation before becoming a main CTA.
- Awards/recognition wording on the chef page is specific and should only be used exactly, with permission/rights confirmed.
- Any third-party press imagery or logos need explicit rights before use.

## Decision options

1. **Approve Bamzi seed** — recommended. Strong visual/story lead for a premium, reservation-led preview once MC seeding/provisioning is available.
2. **Approve pre-MC preview only if Ethan wants a speculative showpiece** — viable because official evidence is strong, but keep it internal until owner facts/photo rights are confirmed.
3. **Hold behind easier broken-site leads** if the lane needs quick “obvious website problem” wins; this is more of a premium repositioning sell than a simple broken-site rescue.
