ABOUT US
A FUSION TWIST ON INDIAN CUISINE AT THE SECRET KITCHEN

The Secret Kitchen (TSK) is a premium dining destination with a legacy that spans across India, Australia, Canada and now USA. Founded by Chef Aanal Kotak, The Secret Kitchen is a unique culinary experience that blends the rich heritage of Indian cuisine with contemporary global flavors. With multiple locations in India, Australia & Canada, we are now thrilled to bring our exceptional dining experience to United States of America.
