## Bamzi route lock

Use **Bamzi** as the single base archetype.

Why Bamzi fits:

- The restaurant’s public identity is theatrical and cinematic: royal-kitchen inspiration, handmade spices, dramatic dessert, jewel-tone dining room, and a U.S. debut story.
- The homepage already leans toward poster-like typography and dark visual rhythm; Bamzi can make that intentional instead of leaving it as sparse/fragmented sections.
- The conversion path is premium dinner reservations, not fast casual ordering. Bamzi can hold a strong Reserve CTA while still giving the menu enough sensory pacing.

Do **not** reroute to:

- **Roma**: too restrained and quiet for this concept’s maximalist interiors and theatrical menu moments.
- **Heaven Palate**: too classic-luxury/steakhouse; would flatten the modern Indian/global-spice identity.
- **Cuisine**: too warm/casual; useful for menu clarity, but not the structural base.

## Recommended homepage structure

1. **Hero / Reserve poster**
   - Dark, cinematic, centered like a restaurant poster.
   - Primary copy direction: “Modern Indian fine dining arrives in Schaumburg” / “Flavors of the world, from India to America.”
   - CTAs: Reserve Now, Explore Menu, Directions.
   - Keep address and dinner hours close enough to reduce friction.

2. **Chef Aanal story**
   - Chef/founder/owner credibility, handmade spices, royal-kitchen inspiration, international concept.
   - Use one strong quote from the official chef page if included: “Food should be an experience...”
   - Keep awards exact and sourced if used; do not turn them into vague “award-winning” claims without the official wording.

3. **Signature menu journey**
   - Convert the full menu into curated chapters instead of a long item dump.
   - Suggested grouped feature cards:
     - Street food reimagined: Avocado Dahi Puri, Parmesan Cheese Pav Bhaji, The Dhokla Fondue.
     - First Plate / tandoor drama: Thecha Paneer Tikka, Tandoor Ke Phool, Punjabi Kukkad Tikka, Nawabi Lamb Chop Tajdar.
     - Royal mains: Burrata Cheese Makhani, #TSKFIED Butter Chicken Delhi Style, Nalli Gosht Ki Nihari, Awadhi Gosht Biryani.
     - Sweet theatre: Gulab Jamun Flambé, Kesar Mango Rasmalai, Chef Aanal’s Autograph Dessert.
   - Link to the full official menu; do not reproduce every item above the fold.

4. **Dining-room / photo moment section**
   - Use the press/evidence direction as visual strategy: jewel tones, brass, emerald, crimson, throne/photo moment, flamingo/onxy bar cues.
   - If builder uses placeholder imagery, it must be clearly replaceable and not imply false ownership/photo rights.

5. **Experience / occasions**
   - Dinner date, celebrations, group dinners, private dining inquiry, premium vegetarian/non-veg table experience.
   - Keep private dining language careful until capacities and policies are confirmed.

6. **Visit / reserve block**
   - Hours, address, phone, email, GetSeat reservation CTA, Google Maps/directions.
   - Mobile sticky CTA: Reserve / Call / Directions.

7. **Footer / social proof**
   - Official socials, India/Australia/Canada/USA presence, contact details.
   - Any press logos/articles only if usage is legally/owner-approved; otherwise mention in internal pitch docs, not public preview.
