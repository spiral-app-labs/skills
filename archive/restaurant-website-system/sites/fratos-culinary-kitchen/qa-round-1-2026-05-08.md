## Findings and fixes

### Finding 1 — Mobile quick-link rail could clip on narrow viewports

- Evidence: first mobile QA screenshot showed the rightmost quick link visually clipped.
- Fix: changed mobile nav from horizontal overflow scrolling to a wrapped, centered pill rail in `components/TopNavSimple.tsx`.
- Verification: `fratos-qa1-home-mobile-after-nav-fix-390x844-2026-05-08.png` shows clean wrapped pills with no clipping.

### Finding 2 — A few marketing claims were stronger than needed for source-safe QA

- Evidence: vision QA flagged `Illinois' Greatest Mozzarella Stick` and `halal wings` as claims to verify before launch.
- Fix: softened hero headline to `The Giant Mozzarella Stick Worth the Drive` and changed `halal wings` to source-safe `halal options` / `wings or tenders` language in `content.example.ts`.
- Verification: updated mobile screenshot shows softened headline and no hard superlative.

### Finding 3 — Concierge visibility needed post-fix screenshot proof

- Evidence: concierge was added during the gate and needed visible entry screenshots.
- Fix: kept the helper visible outside the reveal wrapper, captured desktop/mobile concierge screenshots, and verified the safety copy.
- Verification: `fratos-qa1-concierge-mobile-after-nav-fix-390x844-2026-05-08.png` shows the verified-facts helper, limitations, and large CTAs.
