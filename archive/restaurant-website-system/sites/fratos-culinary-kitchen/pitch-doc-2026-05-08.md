# Frato's Culinary Kitchen Pitch Doc

## 60-Second Read

Frato's already has the hardest part: real demand, a memorable signature item, deep ordering/catering infrastructure, and public proof around giant mozzarella sticks, one-pound slices, wings, burgers, and fresh-made comfort food. The current site is active and useful, but it asks mobile guests to sort through a long promotional stack before they can confidently order, get directions, or route catering. The prototype keeps the existing OrderStart and catering paths intact, then turns the owned site into a cleaner pre-order decision path: order first, proof fast, signatures upfront, mobile actions always near the thumb.

## Call Opener

"Frato's doesn't need a prettier brochure — it needs the first 30 seconds online to feel as strong as the giant mozzarella stick people already drive for."

## What We Fixed

- **Order-first first fold:** The new hero makes `Order Online` the dominant action while keeping menu browsing available.
- **Guest proof that feels alive:** Anonymous Google review fragments scroll in a dark proof band, emphasizing cheese pull, giant mozz, fresh portions, ghost pepper wings, pizza, burgers, and vibe.
- **Mobile decision path:** The top chrome now covers the common mobile jobs: order, menu, catering, directions, and call.
- **Signature curation:** Instead of dumping the whole menu, the prototype leads with the items people remember and review.
- **Preserve-stack handoffs:** OrderStart and FratosCatering.com stay in place; the prototype improves the owned path around them instead of pretending to replace operations.
- **Safe helper for common questions:** A visible Frato's site helper answers ordering, catering, hours, directions, and dietary handoff questions without fake prices, orders, refunds, or guarantees.

## Demo Path

1. **Open the homepage first fold.** Point to the signature headline, Google proof, `Since 1975`, and the red `Order Online` CTA.
2. **Scroll to signatures and deals.** Show how giant mozzarella stick, one-pound slice, Burger of the Month, wings/gyro/catering lanes make the huge menu easier to choose from.
3. **Show the review carousel.** Use the anonymous Google fragments as proof that the new site is built from real guest language, not generic pizza copy.
4. **Open the site helper.** Show the verified-facts helper for order, catering, hours, directions, and dietary handoffs.
5. **Switch to mobile.** Show the sticky order CTA and quick links for Menu, Catering, Helper, Directions, and Call.
6. **Click provider handoffs.** Open OrderStart for ordering and the catering inquiry path to prove the current revenue systems are preserved.

## Do Not Overclaim

- Do not say the prototype replaces OrderStart, catering operations, delivery routing, or live menu management.
- Google rating/review count should be rechecked before a live owner-facing demo if the number must be current to the minute.
- Do not promise live hours, delivery radius, prices, catering availability, or halal/allergen guarantees from the prototype or helper alone.
- No public preview URL has been packaged yet in this local gate; current evidence is local screenshots and build output.

## Close

"The goal is simple: keep the systems Frato's already uses, but make the owned site turn more hungry mobile visitors into orders, calls, directions, and catering inquiries."
