## Route decision

Choose exactly one route: `pepper-01`.

Frato's is not a reservation-led dining room. It is an order-first, hype-driven, fast-casual comfort-food restaurant with pizza slices, giant mozzarella sticks, burgers, wings, gyros, online ordering, catering, and a playful dine-in/gaming environment. The live site and Google reviews both prove that conversion should prioritize ordering, signature items, and trust quickly.

## Intake summary

- Cuisine: pizza, wings, burgers, gyros, mac and cheese, comfort food; halal wings/tenders noted on official site.
- Service type: counter / takeout / delivery / casual dine-in / catering.
- Price point: $10–20 per Google capture.
- Atmosphere: playful, casual, chill, family-friendly, games/slots while waiting.
- Visual tone: bright, bold, dish-forward; should feel fun and craveable rather than formal.
- Reservation intensity: none; order/call/directions/catering are the priority paths.
- Revenue model: online order, pickup/delivery, walk-ins, catering/corporate lunches.
- Day-part: lunch/dinner/all-day casual.
- Destination vs neighborhood: both; review evidence shows people drive in for viral/signature items.
- Occasion: family lunch/dinner, cheat-day comfort food, office/corporate catering, social food run.
- Photography tier: public/live site has strong food imagery but mobile composition is dense; template should emphasize product cards and signature dishes.
- Conversion priorities: Order online, call, directions, catering.

## Why `pepper-01` fits

- Built for pizza/wings/burger/slice-shop economics and order-primary conversion.
- Candy-color deal grid and product-card sections can spotlight giant mozzarella sticks, one-pound pizza slices, burger of the month, wings, gyros, and catering packs.
- Bright, playful tone matches the review themes: giant cheese pulls, huge portions, games, chill staff, and internet-buzz comfort food.
- Single-page menu anchor + persistent Order Now CTA fixes the current site's long/mobile-dense ordering friction.

## Rejected alternatives

1. `plate-01` — good for modern casual bistros, but too restrained/menu-forward for Frato's viral, oversized, order-first energy.
2. `bramble-01` — captures games/bar personality, but would over-index nightlife/bar atmosphere and underplay pizza/wings/order conversion.
3. `gusto-01` — testimonial/trust-led Italian works for trattorias, but Frato's is broader comfort food and not reservation/date-night Italian.
4. `bamzi-01` — saturated/chef-forward could work visually, but the route is more pan-Asian/Latin restaurant than direct pizza/takeout.

## Modifiers

- Palette: keep saturated Pepper energy, bias red/yellow/orange with black/white contrast; allow one green accent for fresh/halal/wings cues.
- Typography: keep Cherry Bomb One wordmark discipline; use heavy sans headlines for order-first clarity.
- CTA: persistent `Order Online`; secondary `Call` / `Get Directions`; catering CTA where relevant.
- Menu: product-card grid + deal/signature stack; no fake prices unless verified from official/order source.
- Proof: Google review summary, 4.2 / 554 reviews, signature-topic chips, customer quote snippets.
- Motion: playful but lightweight; no heavy animation that hurts mobile speed.
- Gallery: dish-forward; use official/live-site public images as evidence cues, not fabricated claims.

## Avoided patterns

- No reservation widget or fine-dining language.
- No fake awards, fake chef claims, fake menu items, or fake prices.
- No generic "authentic Italian" framing; Frato's identity is comfort-food creativity and culinary-student training.
- No stock-review quotes pretending to be real customers beyond summarized Google review evidence.
- Do not bury Order Online below long story content.
