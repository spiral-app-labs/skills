# Frato's Culinary Kitchen — Lead-fit qualification — 2026-05-08

## Decision

**Build.** Frato's Culinary Kitchen passes the lead-fit gate for a **Cuisine**-archetype speculative website. The public reality is a real Schaumburg casual pizza/burger/wing kitchen with a specific student-chef/catering story, rich menu/pricing, recognizable media hooks, direct order/carryout conversion, and enough current-site clutter/mobile weakness to make a redesign pitch concrete.

**Recommended template register:** **Cuisine** — warm, family/casual neighborhood clarity. Use a craveable food-first hero, direct order/call/directions CTAs, menu highlights, Frato's student-chef story, specials/loyalty, halal options, and catering proof. Do **not** route to upscale/luxury or sushi/high-design archetypes.

**Primary conversion path:** Order online through OrderStart / call for pickup, delivery, curbside, or dine-in.

**Secondary paths:** directions/call, catering via Frato's Personal Chef Catering, specials/loyalty offers, halal menu discovery.

**Why-switch sentence:** If Frato's saw our redesign, the specific reason they would switch is that their current WordPress/Nexus site has the right proof—scratch-made menu, media mentions, student-chef story, specials, catering, and ordering—but it buries that proof in a cluttered, dated, mobile-cramped experience instead of making the order path and Frato's story obvious in the first few seconds.

**Google rating/review count:** not directly verified; do not use a Google rating/review count in sales copy until captured from Google Business Profile.

## 7 checks

1. **Visual reality — Pass for Cuisine; do not over-register.** Official/public images show pizzas, burgers, wings, gyros, massive mozzarella sticks, cheese-pull food, a chef/cook filmed at the grill, catering/buffet trays near windows, glass storefront/logo doors, red/black/white casual interior elements, and a Frato's wall/mural. The room and food read as playful casual comfort-food/pizza kitchen, not fine dining.
2. **Menu pricing register — Pass.** Official menu prices sit in casual $/$$ territory: 3-cheese pizzas $15.00-$26.75, creative pizzas $22-$38, 1-pound slices around $4.50-$4.99, specialty burgers $13.50-$15.99, mozzarella logs $7.25/$11.25, tenders $9.99/$14.99, gyros $8.99/$11.50, salads $9.99-$11.99, desserts/shakes $5.99-$8.99. RestaurantGuru also reports a $10-$20 price range. Cuisine matches; upscale templates would overshoot.
3. **Review tone — Pass.** Public review/listing language is casual, neighborhood, and craveable-food oriented: Restaurantji favorites include handmade parmesan mozzarella sticks, halal chicken tenders, gyro sandwich, 1-pound sausage slice, burger of the month, and chicken sandwich; RestaurantGuru mentions mozzarella sticks, boneless wings, chicken gyro, brownies, cheesecakes, whiskey/beer, knowledgeable staff, service, reasonable prices, modern decor, and cozy atmosphere; official testimonials talk about a neighborhood pizza joint, hot cheesy pizza, friendly staff, catering trays, and party pizza. Yelp search snippets show many photos/reviews and lunch/burger praise. Google rating/review count remains unverified.
4. **Current-site quality — Pass for build case; about 5/10.** Desktop has phone, address, hours, menu, order links, media mentions, and active April 2026 posts, so it is not broken. The weakness is that the homepage is long/noisy, WordPress/Nexus-template driven, CTA-heavy, and scattered across many pages. On mobile, the core logo/name/phone/address/order CTA appears, but the cuisine descriptor is cramped/partly lost above the fold, an image alt says “Image is not available,” and many CTAs compete before menu/story clarity. The menu page is rich and price-complete, but ordering redirects externally to OrderStart and the footer exposes a “Pizzeria WordPress Theme” link.
5. **Why-switch — Pass.** The switch reason is specific: Frato's has unusually good proof for a casual restaurant, but the current site does not package it into a fast, mobile-first order/story/menu experience.
6. **Switching cost / inertia — Moderate.** Current stack appears to be WordPress/Nexus, external OrderStart ordering/menu, active specials/blog posts, and a separate FratosCatering.com path. No reservation dependency is visible. A build should preserve OrderStart, phone, directions, and catering links rather than pitch a full operational migration.
7. **Would-I-eat-here test — Pass if restrained.** A Cuisine hero around scratch-made Schaumburg pizza, giant mozzarella sticks, burgers/wings, student-chef training, and direct ordering matches public photos and menu. A luxury reservation-led hero would misrepresent the business.

## Template register recommendation

- **Archetype:** Cuisine
- **Why:** Frato's needs approachable clarity and conversion, not prestige. Cuisine can make the restaurant feel custom and appetizing while keeping the casual neighborhood reality honest.
- **Do not use:** Heaven Palate/Roma/Qitchen (too upscale or wrong category), Bamzi (too nightlife/cinematic unless later evidence centers gaming/bar), Bramble (bar-first mismatch).
