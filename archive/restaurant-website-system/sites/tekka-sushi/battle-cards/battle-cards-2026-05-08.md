## Owner objections + replies

### “We already have online ordering.”
Yes — the redesign keeps that. The problem is not that ordering exists; it is that the owned site does not make the order path trustworthy or obvious from the menu experience. The new site points customers to ordering faster.

### “The menu prices change.”
That is why the preview avoids hard-coding exact prices except captured public examples. It uses menu anchors and pushes current pricing/availability to the live ordering path.

### “We do reservations by phone.”
The redesign respects that. It removes the fake form pattern and makes the phone handoff explicit.

### “We do not want to look too fancy.”
This is a de-ceremonialized Qitchen route: modern and precise like a sushi restaurant, but order-forward and local. No omakase, Michelin, tasting menu, or fake luxury claims.

### “Can the concierge answer everything?”
No, and it should not. It only answers verified menu/location/order/hours questions and routes allergens, availability, large parties, special requests, and holiday hours to phone.
