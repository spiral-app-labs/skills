# Tekka Sushi — Owner pitch doc — 2026-05-08

## One-line pitch

Tekka already has the food and reviews customers trust; the redesigned site turns the first impression from “template menu I can’t trust” into a polished, order-ready sushi experience.

## The visible problem today

Tekka’s current owned menu page contains placeholder/template content instead of the real sushi menu. The captured audit shows fake item names, lorem-style descriptions, generic categories, template/footer residue, and inconsistent hours/footer content. That is the core owner-visible sales angle: customers who land on the site cannot confidently choose a roll, check prices, order, or call.

## The redesigned direction

- Archetype: **Qitchen / qitchen-01**
- Register: de-ceremonialized modern sushi — premium and precise, but not fake omakase or luxury claims
- Primary conversion path: **Order Online → View Menu → Call → Directions**
- Proof: Google **4.8 / 370 reviews**, dine-in/takeout/delivery, review themes around fresh sushi, presentation, chic room, and friendly service

## Before → after story

### Before

- Fake menu categories and placeholder dishes
- Ordering/reservation paths split across separate pages and providers
- No clear owned-site proof from Google reviews
- Mobile conversion path not prominent
- Non-functional template-like reservation experience would create mistrust

### After

- Menu page uses real/public Tekka anchors: Godzilla, Volcano, Spicy Crispy Tuna, Fire Phoenix, sashimi/nigiri, ramen, lunch specials, Platter C
- Home page surfaces Google proof and immediate Order / Call / Directions actions
- Mobile sticky bar keeps conversion available at all times
- Visit page uses truthful handoffs instead of pretending to hold reservations
- Concierge answers common menu/order/location questions safely and routes unsupported questions to phone fallback

## Top 3 improvements to show the owner

1. **Menu credibility restored** — fake menu residue replaced with verified Tekka menu/review anchors.
2. **More orders and calls** — mobile and desktop now have clear Order, Call, Directions, and Menu paths.
3. **Trust-safe assistance** — concierge answers the questions customers ask before ordering without making fake operational promises.

## What not to claim

- Do not claim online reservations are available; Tekka currently routes reservations/special requests to phone.
- Do not claim omakase, Michelin, chef’s counter, or tasting menu.
- Do not invent exact prices except where captured in public ordering evidence.
- Do not claim the concierge places orders or guarantees hours/allergens/table availability.
