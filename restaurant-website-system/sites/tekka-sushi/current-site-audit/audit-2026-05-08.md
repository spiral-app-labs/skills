## Summary verdict

The current site has enough real business content to be credible on the homepage/reservation page, but the official menu page is a severe trust and conversion failure. A Qitchen-led redesign has a clear owner-visible before/after story: replace template/placeholder menu residue with real sushi menu depth, clean conversion paths, and a premium-but-truthful Japanese dining presentation.

## Findings

### 1) Official menu exposes template/placeholder content

The live `/menu` page shows placeholder categories and fake items instead of Tekka’s actual sushi menu.

Examples captured in the scrape:

- Categories: `STARTERS`, `Drinks`, `Main`, `Dessert`, `Lunch`, `Dinner`
- Placeholder item names: `Pine nut sbrisalona`, `Aenean eu`, `Duis sed aliquet`, `Suspendisse`, `tempus aliquet`, `Cras maximus`
- Placeholder descriptions: `Sed fermentum eros vitae eros`, `Feugiat maximus neque pharetra`, `Proin lacinia nisl ut ultricies posuere nulla`
- Footer residue: `contact@site.com`, Colorlib/template copyright, old Twitter/template content

Business impact: customers who land on the owned menu cannot trust prices, dishes, or restaurant polish.

### 2) Conversion paths are fragmented but salvageable

- Header CTA points to Beyond Menu for ordering.
- Separate `tekkasushiil.com` ordering/discovery path exists from earlier qualification research.
- Reservation page is not an online booking flow; it says to call `1-224-875-7188`.

Build implication: preserve actual order/call paths, but make them explicit: **Order Online**, **Call to reserve**, **Directions**, and a real menu path.

### 3) Homepage has usable trust signals, but they are not integrated with menu conversion

Homepage scrape includes:

- Address: `84 Biesterfield Rd, Elk Grove Village, IL 60007`
- Phone: `1-224-875-7188`
- Email: `mphx007@gmail.com`
- Gift card note
- Customer quote snippets mentioning fresh sushi, presentation, service, and atmosphere

Build implication: these are useful proof points, but should be organized around real menu highlights and clear CTAs.

### 4) Hours/contact inconsistency needs cleanup

The menu footer says `M-Th 10 AM – 10 PM`, while the homepage/reservation page say `M-Th 11 AM – 9:30 PM`. Restaurantji also lists Monday–Thursday `11AM - 9:30PM`.

Build implication: do not hard-code hours until current hours are verified. Prefer “Call to confirm holiday hours” if needed.

### 5) Template residue weakens credibility

The menu page includes Colorlib copyright and leftover social/Twitter sample content, while the reservation/home pages show newer 2024 copyright. This looks like a partially replaced template.

Build implication: pitch should lead with owned-site trust, not just aesthetic modernization.

## Concrete opportunities for the redesign

1. Replace fake menu sections with real sushi/Japanese structure: signature rolls, sushi/sashimi, ramen, platters, lunch/dinner, appetizers, beverages.
2. Give Qitchen-style visual priority to signature rolls and freshness, without claiming omakase/luxury unless later evidence supports it.
3. Create one clean conversion row: Order Online, Call to Reserve, Directions, Menu.
4. Surface address, phone, and verified hours consistently.
5. Remove all template residue and fake content.
6. Use real public favorites from qualification evidence as menu/content anchors: Godzilla Roll, Crazy Tuna Roll, Spicy Crispy Tuna Roll, Yellowtail Hamachi, Fire Phoenix Roll, Regular Sashimi, Spicy Miso Ramen, California Roll.
