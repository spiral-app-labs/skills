## Build thesis

Create a classic, reservation-first luxury steakhouse site that makes Chicago Prime Steakhouse feel like a polished Schaumburg fine-dining institution: prime steaks, private rooms, wine/service, live lounge energy, and George Kalkounos’s hospitality legacy. The preview should make guests want to reserve dinner or inquire about private dining before they get lost in repeated CTAs, PDFs, or template residue.

## Recommended archetype

Use **Heaven Palate**.

Fit rationale:

- Upscale steakhouse / fine dining.
- Reservation-led primary conversion.
- Strong private dining and event revenue path.
- Classic hospitality legacy story.
- Wine/lounge/live entertainment can support the luxury experience without becoming the main archetype.

## Primary conversion hierarchy

1. Reserve a table via OpenTable.
2. Request private dining / events information.
3. Explore the menu / menu PDFs.
4. Call or get directions.
5. Order online through existing provider handoffs where appropriate.

## What a preview should fix visibly

- Remove or avoid current site template residue: bakery placeholders, `555-555-55-55`, slash placeholders, review carousel placeholders, duplicate copyright clutter.
- Make Reserve and Private Dining the clean homepage spine.
- Integrate George Kalkounos’s legacy with restraint and respect.
- Clarify live music as a lounge enhancement, not a brand pivot.
- Turn private dining capacities and room names into a polished events conversion section after owner/currentness confirmation.
- Preserve provider links and official facts.
