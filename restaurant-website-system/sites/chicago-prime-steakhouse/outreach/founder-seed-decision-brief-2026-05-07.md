## Why Heaven Palate, not Bamzi or Bramble

Heaven Palate fits because Chicago Prime’s strongest public cues are classic steakhouse luxury: Reserve-first dinner, private dining, steak/seafood/wine, white-tablecloth expectations, warm service, and a founder/family hospitality legacy.

Bamzi would make the site too trendy and cinematic for a restaurant whose sales story is trust, institution, and private-event confidence. Bramble would over-weight the lounge/live music and push the brand toward nightlife, even though the lounge is a support feature rather than the core conversion. Cuisine would improve clarity but undersell the premium steakhouse occasion. Roma would be too quiet/chef-seasonal. Qitchen would feel too architectural/high-concept.

## What a preview should prove

1. The first screen can immediately feel premium and trustworthy without losing warmth.
2. Reserve and Private Dining can become the obvious commercial spine.
3. Template residue can be removed in a way that visibly upgrades trust.
4. The private dining rooms and event inquiry path can feel like a major revenue line instead of buried detail.
5. The George A. Kalkounos story can support a hospitality legacy module without becoming heavy or exploitative.
6. Live lounge music can add atmosphere without turning the brand into a bar site.
7. Mobile can become a high-converting Reserve / Call / Directions flow.
