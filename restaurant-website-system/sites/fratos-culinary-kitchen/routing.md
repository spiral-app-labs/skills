## Why `pepper-01` fits

Frato's public business reality is not upscale reservation dining. It is a distinctive, casual, order-forward Schaumburg comfort-food kitchen: pizza, 1-pound slices, giant mozzarella sticks, burgers, wings, gyros, halal options, direct OrderStart ordering, phone/carryout, playful dine-in/gaming atmosphere, and catering.

`pepper-01` is the best concrete route because it is the catalog's order-primary pizza/wings/burger/takeout template. Its bright, playful, dish-forward structure can make the giant mozzarella stick and oversized comfort-food proof feel like the site identity instead of burying them in a generic restaurant page. It also matches the primary conversion path: **Order Online → Call → Directions → Catering**, not reservations.

- Review themes support a playful comfort-food route: giant mozzarella stick / cheese pull, oversized scratch comfort food, pizza/burgers/wings/gyros, value/large portions, friendly staff, chill game-filled dine-in atmosphere, and online-order/carryout utility.
- Current-site audit found the main gap is packaging: the existing site has the right proof but is long, cluttered, mobile-heavy, and CTA-dense.

## Rejected alternatives

1. **`plate-01`** — rejected because it is a restrained modern-casual bistro/menu-forward workhorse. Frato's needs louder order-first energy, deal/signature cards, and playful comfort-food identity. Plate risks making the restaurant feel like a generic neighborhood bistro instead of the home of giant mozzarella sticks and 1-pound slices.
2. **`bramble-01`** — rejected because it over-indexes cocktail-bar/day-night hospitality. Frato's has gaming/chill atmosphere, but the revenue reality is pizza/comfort-food orders and catering, not bar/nightlife positioning.
3. **`gusto-01`** — rejected because it is a dark heritage Italian trattoria/date-night route. Frato's has history and Italian-American pizza roots, but the public proof is broader creative comfort food, OrderStart conversion, and viral oversized dishes, not reservation-led trattoria romance.
4. **`bamzi-01`** — rejected because its dark saturated chef-forward architecture is better for pan-Asian/Latin or chef-led sit-down concepts. Frato's does have a culinary-student story, but the primary commercial job is fast order conversion and menu/signature clarity.

## Required modifiers for the `pepper-01` fork

- **Palette:** keep Pepper's bright saturated order-first energy; bias red/yellow/orange with black/white contrast. Use any green accent only as a small freshness/halal/wings cue, not as the main brand.
- **Typography:** keep the playful Cherry Bomb-style wordmark discipline and heavy sans headlines. Do not introduce serif/fine-dining type.
- **CTA hierarchy:** primary `Order Online`; secondary `Call`, `Get Directions`, and `Catering`. No reservation CTA.
- **Menu strategy:** lead with curated signature cards before full menu/order handoff: Giant Mozzarella Stick, 1-Pound Pizza Slice, Burger of the Month, Wings/Tenders including halal options, Gyros, and Catering/Corporate Lunch. Do not publish unverified prices.
- **Proof strategy:** use anonymous aggregate Google proof only: 4.2 stars / 554 reviews, 30 Highest-filter written reviews captured, and theme chips for giant mozz, portions, scratch comfort food, friendly staff, chill games, and value.
- **Story strategy:** use the student-chef / culinary training and `Since 1975` proof as a concise trust band, not a long hero narrative that delays ordering.
- **Motion:** playful lightweight product motion is OK; avoid heavy animation that hurts mobile speed or masks weak content.
- **Mobile:** sticky or repeated mobile actions for Order / Call / Directions / Catering; the current site is very long on mobile, so the fork must shorten decision time.
