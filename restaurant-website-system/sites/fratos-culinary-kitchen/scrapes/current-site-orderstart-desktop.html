Quesabirria Burger - May

AS SEEN ON WGN NEWS & WINDY CITY LIVE!

(Choice of Bacon-Beef, All Beef or Halal Beef Burgers)

Our chefs slow-cook chicken birria from scratch, braised for hours with dried chiles, cumin, & Mexican oregano until it falls apart. We pile it on 2 smoked apple bacon & beef patties with melted Monterey Jack, cilantro, diced onion, & lettuce. Then we soak the grilled Turano bun in homemade consommé

*All Burgers are made Medium Well, We Do Not Cook To Other Temps* Please keep in mind, Smoked Bacon is Pink, but it is Fully Cooked. If You Don't Want Pink, Choose All-Beef

What Is a Group Order?

Now you can place orders easily with your friends, family, and coworkers.

With group ordering, you can create one order as the host, and then share a link to that order with a group of people. Your guests can then add their choices to the order. Everyone will be able to see the order in real-time. As the host, you can also set a spend limit for your guests.

Once everyone has ordered, it is the host's responsbility to complete the order and make the final payment. The order will then be sent through all at once.
