From-Scratch Food. Debt-Free Chefs.

At Frato’s Kitchen (FratosKitchen.com), your meal funds a mission. We recruit local culinary students and train them into working chefs—without college debt. In an industry that leans on freezers, we cook from scratch every day. Our young chefs have competed in culinary competitions and our work’s been featured on Chicago’s Best, WGN News, Windy City Live, Fox News, and more. Frato’s Kitchen is the training ground for Frato’s Personal Chef Catering—the team behind weddings, large corporate events, and full food service for medical facilities and schools. When cooks master the line here, they graduate to careers there.

★ OUR 2-BITE GUARANTEE

We cook from scratch — not factory food. Our culinary students are in training, and mistakes can happen. Take 2 bites — if you’re not happy, bring your dish to the Chef on Duty for a full refund, remake, or store credit. Must return at least 75% of the dish for a full refund. Full details at FratosPizza.com/guarantee
