About Us

The Chef Grill

I’m İbrahim Kelesabdıoglu, the chef and founder of The Chef Grill in Elk Grove Village, IL. With over fifteen years of global culinary experience, I bring the rich traditions of Halal Turkish and Mediterranean cuisine to your table. From charcoal-grilled kebabs to healthy Mediterranean breakfasts, every dish reflects my passion for authentic flavors, fresh ingredients, and creating unforgettable dining experiences.

Open Kitchen Experience

At The Chef Grill in Elk Grove Village, every dish is prepared before your eyes in our open Mediterranean kitchen. We use fresh, non-GMO, halal meats, organic vegetables, and authentic ingredients to create the best Mediterranean dining experience in Elk Grove Village, IL. From charcoal-grilled kebabs to traditional Turkish stews and soups, every recipe celebrates the heart of Halal Turkish and Mediterranean cuisine.

More Than Just Dining

Our open kitchen invites you to watch the art of cooking unfold. Enjoy specialties like homemade manti Turkish dumplings, crispy brick-oven pide (Turkish pizza), and fresh Mediterranean lunch specials prepared with passion. Every meal tells a story of tradition, culture, and flavor.

Halal Commitment

At The Chef Grill in Elk Grove Village, IL, we take pride in offering a 100% halal-certified Mediterranean dining experience. Every meal reflects the highest level of quality, authenticity, and integrity. Our meats are sourced from trusted halal suppliers and are always hormone-free, GMO-free, and naturally raised, giving you complete confidence in every bite. When you visit, you’ll enjoy the best halal Turkish grill made with care and tradition.

What Halal Means to Us
  • Ethically Sourced & Hand-Slaughtered Meats – We serve only halal-certified lamb, beef, and chicken, prepared with strict standards.
  • Pure & Wholesome Ingredients – From Amish butter and non-GMO eggs to fresh vegetables and organic olive oil, our meals are made with premium quality.
  • No Cross-Contamination – Our kitchen follows dedicated halal practices so your Mediterranean food in Elk Grove Village is always authentic and worry-free.

From our charcoal-grilled kebabs and healthy Mediterranean lunches to traditional Turkish soups, stews, and desserts, every dish at The Chef Grill celebrates true halal excellence. Visit us today and enjoy the authentic flavors of halal Mediterranean cuisine, pure, fresh, and unforgettable.
