## 7 Checks

1. Visual reality: Real public listing/photos and the official site point to a downtown Crystal Lake smokehouse: smoked meats, ribs, sandwiches, bar service, and catering. The register should feel local, smoky, unfussy, and food-forward rather than upscale steakhouse or generic pub.
2. Menu pricing register: Starters sit around $7-$15, ribs are $24 half / $34 full, half-pound meats run $12-$18, and signature sandwiches run roughly $16.50-$21. This supports a polished casual BBQ template with strong appetizing product photography and easy ordering/catering paths.
3. Review tone: Public review summaries describe a clean, friendly, welcoming BBQ spot, with frequent praise for brisket, ribs, wings, smoked turkey, cheese fries, sauces, and comfort-food sides. Atmosphere is relaxed and neighborhood-oriented.
4. Current-site quality: The current site has important basics: menu, story, contact, hours, catering form, and Toast ordering. It is still a simple WordPress-style experience with weak conversion hierarchy, limited first-screen food impact, scattered catering/order paths, typo-level polish issues, and little owner-safe sales framing for catering/private events.
5. Why-switch sentence: Moontime would switch if a redesign makes catering and online ordering feel as credible as the BBQ itself, with a stronger first impression, cleaner mobile ordering path, and a catering quote funnel that does more selling before the form.
6. Switching cost: Moderate. The restaurant already uses its own domain and a Toast ordering flow, so the redesign should preserve Toast and the existing catering email/form workflow instead of replacing operations.
7. Would-I-eat-here test: Yes. The proposed hero should be honest to the public evidence: smoked ribs/brisket, sauces, downtown casual room, and a friendly bar/catering posture. Avoid luxury plating or dark cocktail-lounge styling.

## Decision Rationale

Build. The restaurant is real, well-reviewed, and locally recognizable, with enough menu depth and catering value to justify a higher-converting preview. The existing site is functional but not a strong sales asset, so the preview should focus on food credibility, catering conversion, mobile order friction, and neighborhood trust.
