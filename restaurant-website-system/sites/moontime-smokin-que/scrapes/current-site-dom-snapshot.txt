ABOUT mOONTIME
THE MOONTIME
STORY
Our passion for everything BBQ is rivaled only by our passion for customer satisfaction! We’re Heather & Joe Cummings from Crystal Lake, Illinois. We have over 30 years of experience in the service industry and are eager to provide exceptional service to all of our customers, no matter how small or large your event may be!
Here at Moontime Smokin’ Que, it’s all about lovin’ our food and our people. Low n slow is how we do it. Fresh, all wood-fired and made with love is what sets us apart from all others. Our customers become friends, and the love we get from our “Moontimers” is given back through our quality product and personable service.
Moontime Smokin’ Que currently operates out of 88 Railroad Street, Unit A in Crystal Lake, IL. We started running our catering business at our old location at Ralph’s General Store in 2017, expanded to carry-out service, and since have grown to a full service restaurant Wednesday thru Sunday. Our new downtown location has a full bar with signature cocktails, premuim liquors, and a friendly beer list filled with local and regional brewers.
Catering packages

We have catering packages available starting at 20 people to 300 to make your life a little easier, but we are always into being creative to figure out something that works for everybody! Click below to fill out an inquiry form.
