# Moontime Smokin' Que Template Routing

Date: 2026-06-04
Lead ID: 788d0e2a-8fea-4a88-9d28-bbc0263959a6
Selected template: `bramble-01`

## Intake

- Cuisine: BBQ / smokehouse with ribs, brisket, pulled pork, smoked wings, sandwiches, sides, and sauces.
- Service type: Casual full-service plus Toast carryout ordering and catering/event packages.
- Price point: $-$$, with sandwiches around the high teens and ribs/catering as higher-value items.
- Atmosphere: Friendly downtown Crystal Lake smokehouse with a full bar, local-owner story, casual groups, families, and repeat regulars.
- Visual tone: Warm, smoky, neighborhood, food-forward, slightly rustic; not luxury steakhouse or generic pub.
- Reservation/order intensity: Order and catering are primary; dine-in and directions are important; no reservation path was verified.
- Revenue model: Multi-stream local operation: dine-in, carryout, catering, bar/drinks, gift cards/rewards.
- Day-part: Lunch and dinner.
- Destination vs neighborhood role: Local neighborhood draw with enough BBQ credibility to pull diners from nearby towns.
- Occasion: Casual weeknight meals, family dinners, bar nights, birthdays, catering, and smoked turkey/holiday-style orders.
- Photography tier: Public evidence supports real food photography, but the site needs stronger curation and a warmer visual system.
- Conversion priorities: Toast order, catering/contact, menu scanning, call/directions, local trust, and signature menu proof.

## Route

Use `bramble-01` as the primary route.

Bramble is the best match because Moontime is a warm neighborhood hospitality brand with a food program, bar posture, local character, and multiple conversion paths. Its scrapbook/polaroid energy, opening-times structure, dual menu/service split, and warm light-mode register can carry a casual smokehouse without making it feel either too premium or too fast-casual.

## Rejected Alternatives

- `plate-01`: Strong for a modern casual bistro, but too clean and cuisine-neutral for a smoky BBQ brand with bar/catering personality. It would make the site feel more like a generic neighborhood restaurant than a memorable smokehouse.
- `pepper-01`: Good for takeout-first pizza/wings/burger energy, but too candy-colored and deal-driven for Moontime. The restaurant needs order conversion, but not a fast-casual promo site.
- `1776-redesign-01`: Too upscale and reservation-coded. Moontime needs polished trust and food credibility, not a warm fine-dining destination register.

## Modifiers

- Palette: warm smokehouse cream, char, brass/amber, deep sauce red, muted wood tones.
- Typography: friendly display with sturdy sans body; avoid fine-dining serif drama.
- CTA: dual conversion with `Order Online` and `Catering / Events`; secondary `Menu`, `Call`, and `Directions`.
- Menu: category-forward smoked meats, sandwiches, sauces, sides, drinks, and catering hooks. Preserve Toast as the order path.
- Gallery: high-food-impact polaroid/scrapbook treatment with ribs, brisket, wings, sauces, cornbread, and downtown/bar atmosphere.
- Proof: Google rating/review count plus short review phrases around brisket, ribs, sauces, friendly service, and downtown Crystal Lake.
- Heritage: owner story with Heather and Joe Cummings, the 2017 start, and growth from catering/carry-out to full-service downtown bar.
- Motion: subtle smoke/scrapbook drift only; no luxury cinematic motion.

## Required Sections

- Full-bleed warm BBQ hero with Toast order and catering CTAs.
- Signature smokehouse proof strip: brisket, ribs, wings, pulled pork, sauces, cornbread/beans.
- Menu path that makes carryout and dine-in decisions fast.
- Catering/event band for 20-300 person packages and holiday smoked-turkey-style orders.
- Local owner story and downtown Crystal Lake location.
- Review-backed trust section using the captured Highest-filter Google review themes.
- Hours, phone, address, directions, Toast order, and catering email/form.
- Footer with social links, gift cards/rewards if preserved from the current site.

## Avoided Patterns

- No reservation-first nav.
- No upscale steakhouse darkness or ceremonial fine-dining copy.
- No generic "best BBQ" claims without tying them to review/menu evidence.
- No fake menu items, fake owner claims, fake delivery promises, or invented private-event infrastructure.
- Do not hide ordering, catering, hours, or location below decorative sections.

## Register Risk

The main risk is overshooting into a cocktail-bar template because `bramble-01` has bar DNA. Keep BBQ, catering, and food proof dominant; let the bar support the experience rather than define it.
