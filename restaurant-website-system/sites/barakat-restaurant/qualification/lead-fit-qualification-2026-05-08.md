## 7-check lead-fit gate

1. **Visual reality** — Directionally pass, with audit follow-up required. Public marketplace/listing evidence points to a real dine-in restaurant with a distinctive Central Asian / European menu story. The build should not over-luxury-position until browser/photo audit confirms room, plate, and guest register.
2. **Menu pricing register** — Pass for a mid-to-upper local dinner register. Public snippets/research cite plov, manti, lagman, grilled steaks, and fresh pasta; use a cinematic but accessible register rather than fine-dining exclusivity.
3. **Review tone** — Enough to proceed to build qualification, but full Google/Yelp review packet remains a later canonical gate. Existing public surfaces are sufficient to identify the opportunity; do not invent review quotes.
4. **Current-site quality** — Strong pass. Web fetch of `https://barakatplace.com/` exposed only `Barakat` plus an `Edit with Lovable` badge, with no readable menu, reservation, order, address, hours, phone, or story content. Current-site score: **1/10** for conversion clarity.
5. **Why-switch sentence** — If Barakat saw the redesign, the specific reason they would switch is that their current official site looks like an unfinished AI/Lovable shell while their real restaurant already has enough reserve/order demand and cuisine story to deserve a trust-building, conversion-oriented site.
6. **Switching cost / inertia** — Moderate. Reservation/order intent appears to live on OpenTable/Toast rather than a fully integrated owned site, so a preview can preserve those links instead of forcing a platform migration. Need verify current owner preference and provider links before final delivery.
7. **Would-I-eat-here / pitch sanity** — Pass if routed as an atmospheric, story-rich local restaurant rather than inaccessible fine dining. The likely direction should make Central Asian / European hospitality feel vivid and approachable.

## Recommended template register

**Bamzi** — cinematic dark/light story pacing fits the distinctive Central Asian + European dinner concept better than a generic neighborhood layout. Do not use Qitchen/Heaven Palate unless later photo/menu evidence proves a much higher fine-dining register.
