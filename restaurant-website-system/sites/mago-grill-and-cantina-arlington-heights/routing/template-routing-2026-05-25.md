# Mago Grill & Cantina - Template Routing

Captured: 2026-05-25
Workflow step: template_routing
Selected template: `bramble-01`

## Intake

- Cuisine/service: Mexican and Latin cantina with chef-driven dishes, tableside guacamole, moles, tacos, seafood, steaks, margaritas, tequila flights, online ordering, reservations, catering, and group dining.
- Price/register: $$ to low $$$. The route needs to feel polished and lively, not fast-casual taco-shop and not fine-dining ceremonial.
- Atmosphere: downtown Arlington Heights dining room in the Metropolis Performing Arts Centre building, sidewalk seating, two dining rooms, group energy, tequila/cocktail pull, and a location-specific night-out story.
- Revenue model: multi-stream - reservations, online ordering, private events, catering, gift cards, walk-ins, tequila/cocktail discovery, and repeat local/group visits.
- Conversion priority: first screen should make `Reserve`, `Order Online`, `Call/Directions`, and `Group Dining` legible while selling the food/drink story faster than the current location page.
- Review evidence: Google Highest packet captured 30 written reviews. Strongest themes are Mexican food, tableside guacamole, moles/signature dishes, margaritas/tequila, service, special occasions, downtown room/vibe, repeat visits, and value.

## Chosen Route

Use `bramble-01` because Mago's strongest rebuild angle is social cantina hospitality with a real food program, not just a cuisine-color identity. Bramble's hero slideshow, warm light-mode rhythm, scrapbook/photo strip, opening-times band, and food/drinks split can carry Mago's multiple draws: tableside guacamole, moles, margaritas, tequila depth, downtown dinner, sidewalk seating, and private/group dining.

The route should keep the restaurant name as the visual anchor, then use the eyebrow/subcopy to say what the current page hides: chef-driven Mexican cooking, 200-plus tequilas, downtown Arlington Heights energy, and group-ready hospitality.

## Rejected Alternatives

- `bamzi-01`: the router's generic Mexican/Latin fallback is plausible, but Bamzi's dark-canvas pan-Asian chef template would need heavier structural and botanical rework. It risks making Mago feel like a cuisine-color swap instead of a lively cantina with food, tequila, events, and warm hospitality.
- `plate-01`: strong for a menu-forward neighborhood bistro, but it flattens Mago into food browsing and underplays margaritas, tequila flights, group dining, table-side moments, and the social room.
- `labrisa-01`: useful for multi-service coastal/Mediterranean venues, but its paper-and-ink European/coastal register would misread Mago's Aztec-accented Mexican cantina energy.

## Modifiers

- Palette: keep Bramble's warm cream/light-mode hospitality base, then use restrained agave green, paprika red, marigold, and deep mole-brown accents. Avoid candy-bright taqueria colors and moody black speakeasy tones.
- Typography: large restaurant-name wordmark with a delicate serif or refined display face; readable modern sans for hours, menu, events, and mobile CTAs. The name remains the h1, not a tagline.
- CTA system: hero CTAs should include `Reserve`, `Order Online`, and a secondary `Group Dining` or `Plan a Fiesta`. Mobile sticky actions should prioritize call, directions, reserve, order, and events.
- Menu strategy: split into Food and Drinks/Tequila, with feature chips for tableside guacamole, moles, skirt steak, margarita flight, tequila selection, sangria, tacos, and specials pulled from review/menu evidence.
- Gallery/proof: use Bramble scrapbook/polaroid language for guacamole, margaritas, moles, dining room, Fiesta Room/group setup, patio/sidewalk seating, and customer photo energy. Put Google proof near the top without making the site a review wall.
- Group/events: include a clear Fiesta Room/private-events block using verified capacity language only where sourced: Arlington Heights Fiesta Room for 20-100 guests, family-style menus, bar packages, and event planning assistance.
- Owner/chef story: surface Chef Juan Luis Gonzalez, Chef Ric Munoz, Grandma Paula family recipes, moles, and 200-plus tequila program as the credibility layer.
- Motion: moderate slideshow, photo-strip drift, and warm section switching. Avoid luxury cinematic restraint and heavy decorative animation.

## Avoided Patterns

- Do not turn Mago into a fast-casual taco shop or a loud candy-color takeout site.
- Do not route as dark ceremonial fine dining; the evidence supports lively hospitality and multiple conversion paths.
- Do not bury order, group dining, or catering behind atmosphere-only storytelling.
- Do not invent owner/founding-year claims that were not publicly verified.
- Do not use generic AI Mexican copy; seed all prose from the audit, menu, Google review packet, and owner/site language.

## Register Risk

The main risk is choosing a template purely from cuisine. `bamzi-01` fits the generic Mexican/Latin table, but Mago's owner-sellable gap is the current page's underplayed cantina rhythm: food, tequila, social proof, groups, and downtown night-out energy. `bramble-01` carries that multi-stream hospitality story with less structural strain.
