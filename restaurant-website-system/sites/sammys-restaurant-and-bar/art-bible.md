# Sammy's Restaurant & Bar Art Bible

## Source

The active visual source is `public/images/raw/inspo.png`: a dark Huntley tavern bar with amber beer light, glossy wood, a huge pretzel, cold beer, fish fry in the background, cream block lettering, thin amber rules, and an outlined amber call pill.

## Register

Sammy's is a hometown bar and all-day diner, not a bistro, steakhouse, cocktail lounge, or polished hospitality group. The site should feel like warm wood, late-night beer light, Friday fish fry, karaoke, and regulars who know the bartender.

## Palette

- Page canvas: near-black tavern room, `#090503`.
- Section surfaces: smoked dark wood, `#160D08`.
- Primary text: warm cream, `#F7F0E4`.
- Muted text: beer-lit tan, `#BDAF9A`.
- Accent: amber sign light, `#D9A23A`.
- Hover accent: brighter amber, `#E6B84D`.
- Dividers: low-contrast brown hairlines, `#3B2A1C`.

## Typography

- Display: Oswald / condensed sign-painter sans, uppercase or near-uppercase for major headings.
- Body: Urbanist, compact and readable.
- Hero/title treatment: oversized condensed cream wordmark, tight line height, no negative letter spacing.
- Section headings should feel like bar signage, not editorial bistro headlines.
- Body copy remains calm and legible; do not turn paragraphs into all caps.

## Components

- CTAs use rounded amber pill geometry with dark text when filled, or amber outline with amber text when outlined.
- Cards are dark smoked surfaces with amber/brown borders, not cream cards.
- Photos sit in dark frames with soft amber-toned shadows.
- Dividers are thin amber-brown rules.
- Status pills can keep green open-state dots, but should sit on dark smoked surfaces.

## Section Rhythm

The hero is full-bleed and dark. Downstream sections should continue the same tavern world, alternating near-black open bands with smoked-wood cards. Avoid sudden cream pages, bistro whitespace, and generic light template panels.

## Is

- Dark tavern, amber beer glow, glossy bar wood.
- Big condensed sign lettering.
- Cream text on smoky backgrounds.
- Concrete Sammy's proof: fish fry, karaoke, all-day breakfast, huge pretzel, 8 AM-2 AM hours.
- Phone-first conversion.

## Is Not

- Cream bistro landing page.
- Terracotta Italian restaurant palette.
- Thin fashion typography.
- Fake reservations or upscale chef language.
- White cards, pale peach buttons, or generic blog-template polish.
