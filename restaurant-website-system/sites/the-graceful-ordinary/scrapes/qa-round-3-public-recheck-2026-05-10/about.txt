Our Story — The Graceful Ordinary | St. Charles, IL The Graceful Ordinary Menu Our Story Contact Reserve Our Story The Graceful Ordinary How We Began A labor of love in the community they call home For Chef Chris Curren and his wife Megan, The Graceful Ordinary opened in late summer 2021 as a new opportunity to do what they love in the community where they live — downtown St. Charles, IL on the banks of the Fox River. The result is an elevated riverfront concept that earned AAA Three-Diamond recognition — a refined American tavern with a rotating seasonal menu delivered from a wood-burning hearth, a thoughtfully curated wine program, and inventive cocktails. Grace in the ordinary. That philosophy shapes every decision — from the sourcing of ingredients to the warmth of the welcome. “There is grace in the ordinary.” “A AAA Three-Diamond restaurant reminiscent of a town tavern in Colonial times — featuring creative, authentic menus delivered fresh from a wood-burning hearth.” — TripAdvisor Recognition The Team The people behind the experience Executive Chef & Owner Chris Curren Award-winning chef and Fox Valley native. General Manager Jack Sonin A lifelong Fox Valley native, Jack earned his Introductory Sommelier Certificate at 21. Formerly of Michelin-Starred Oriole, Kumiko, and acclaimed Tied House — now curating TGO's thoughtful new and old-world wine program. Pastry Chef Martina Preradovic Born and raised in suburban Chicago's Fox Valley, Martina brings a predisposition toward the creative to TGO's pastry program — inventive yet approachable interpretations of classic desserts that mirror Chef Curren's refined rustic style. What Drives Us Ingredients first. Always. Seasonal & Rotating Our menus change weekly, anchored by what is freshest and best each season. Every visit brings something new. Wood-Fired Technique The hearth is the heart of our kitchen. Live fire brings depth, char, and character that no other cooking method can replicate. Community Rooted We live here.
