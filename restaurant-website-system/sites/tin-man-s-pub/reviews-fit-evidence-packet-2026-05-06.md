## Bramble fit verdict

**Bramble is only a partial fit.** Tin Man’s is a sports pub / pool hall / old-school corner bar, not a polished cocktail lounge or destination nightlife brand. A normal Bramble page would oversell the concept and could make the pitch feel fake. The only viable version is a stripped-down Bramble pub/corner-bar page with:

- above-fold: Tin Man’s Pub name, “pool, cold beer, breakfast/bar food, Fox River Grove” positioning
- CTAs: Call / Directions / View menu
- trust proof: no inflated review wall; maybe one cautious “local pool + late hours” line
- sections: hours/location, pool tables, bar food/menu categories, parking, photos, contact
- hard bans: no fake awards, no invented review counts, no upscale cocktail copy, no political/identity-sensitive language, no staff names unless owner-confirmed
