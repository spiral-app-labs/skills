FOUNDER & OWNER

Chef Aanal Kotak is a celebrated name in the Indian culinary world, known for her journey from a passionate 12-year-old to a renowned celebrity chef. Her love for experimenting with ingredients and flavors has led to the creation of unforgettable dishes. Inspired by royal kitchens, Aanal mastered the art of crafting her own spices from edible flowers, petals, and other unique sources. To this day, she personally creates all the spices for The Secret Kitchen, bringing a distinct, authentic touch to every meal.
